How to Use the Website

Follow these steps to report a damaged school facility or equipment.

Student Reporting Guide

  1. Open Home / Dashboard. This is the main page where you can submit a fault report.
  2. Enter the location. Include a room number, floor, laboratory, or other clear location.
  3. Select a category. Choose the appropriate electronic equipment, utility, general equipment, or school facility category.
  4. Describe the problem. Explain what is damaged and how it affects its use.
  5. Choose a severity. Select Critical, High, Moderate, or Low according to the severity guide.
  6. Add a photo if available. Never put yourself at risk to take a picture.
  7. Submit the report. Review your information and press Submit Fault Report.
  8. Check Report History. Review the reports saved on your current device.
  9. Use Feedback and Settings. Share suggestions or change between light and dark mode.
If a problem presents immediate danger, notify a teacher or school authority directly.
